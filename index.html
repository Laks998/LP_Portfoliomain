About Me

A little about the person behind the work.

I started out studying architecture, spent a few years figuring out what I actually wanted to design, and eventually found my way to UX. I did my Master’s in Human-Computer Interaction in the UK, and now I spend my time thinking about how to make complicated things feel a little simpler. These days, I am also learning more about accessibility in design, because good experiences should work for more people.

Outside of design, I do embroidery, rock climbing, and a little Spanish. I have three dogs at home, occasionally touch grass, and am still getting reacquainted with snow. Basically, I like making things, learning things, and occasionally remembering that screens are not the whole world.
