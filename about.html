Building Quippy

Quippy started as a fun project at a Figma Makethon: a browser extension for instant text conversions and lookups. After leaving Consultcraft in early October, I decided to build it out properly and have been working on it full-time since.

Launched it in January for initial testing, and it's now live for everyone! Highlight text, press Ctrl+Shift+Q, and get definitions, currency conversions, time zones, calculations, all without breaking your flow.

Try it: Chrome Web Store
